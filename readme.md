Twin Calculator
=======================

**Description:** Two calculators side-by-side on one widget"

[![twincalc.png](https://i.postimg.cc/wB8GSqvX/twincalc.png)](https://postimg.cc/xX55mSL8)


One of those things you wonder about until you actually see it.
Not only are there two calculators, but you can send results between them.
Great for what-if calculations.

Packaged as a Desktop applet. 

You may need to resize the app to see everything.

Adapted and packaged for Haiku by Michel Clasquin-Johnson. Tested on Haiku R1/beta6 x86_64.

**Requires:** qt5  
**Original Source:** https://github.com/TwIntern24/twin-calculator
